Nutrients

What are Nutrients?

Nutrients are essential substances found in food that our bodies need to grow, produce energy, repair tissues, and stay healthy. Getting the right balance of nutrients helps support your immune system and keeps your organs working properly.

Types of Nutrients

  • Carbohydrates
  • Proteins
  • Fats
  • Vitamins
  • Minerals
  • Water

Foods Rich in Vitamins

  1. Oranges and citrus fruits (Rich in Vitamin C)
  2. Carrots and sweet potatoes (Rich in Vitamin A)
  3. Spinach and leafy greens (Rich in Vitamin K and Folate)
  4. Salmon and eggs (Rich in Vitamin D)
  5. Almonds and sunflower seeds (Rich in Vitamin E)
