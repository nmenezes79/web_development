🌦️ Weather vs. Climate 🌍

Let's compare the weather we see every day with the pattern that stick around for years.

🌦️ What's Weather?

Weather is what's happening in the atmosphere at a certain place and time, like the temperature, rain, wind, and clouds.

🌦️ Different types of Weather

  • ☀️ Sunny
  • ⛈️ Rainy
  • 💨 Windy
  • ❄️ Snowy
  • 🌪️ Tornado
  • 🔥 Heatwave

🌍 What's Climate?

Climate is the usual weather pattern in a place over many years.

It covers things like typical temperatures, rainfall, humidity, sunshine, and seasons.

🌍 Different types of Climates

  • 🌴 Tropical
  • 🏜️ Desert
  • ❄️ Polar
  • 🌲 Continental
  • 🌧️ Temperate
