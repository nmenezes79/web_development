Today's Special: Vegetable Biryani

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Basmati Rice
  • 1 Cups Mixed Vegetable
  • 2 Cups Ghee or Butter
  • 1 Cups Cumin Seeds
  • Salt To Add Taste

Steps

Step 1: Wash and soak rice for 30 minutes.

Step 2: Heat ghee and add cumin seeds.

Step 3: Add vegetable, cook for 5 minutes.

Step 4: Add rice, and salt. Cook on low heat for 20 minutes.

Step 5: Fluff with a fork and serve hot!
