Weather vs. Climate

What is a Weather?

Weather is the mix of events that happen each day in our atmosphere. Weather is different in different parts of the world and changes over minutes, hours, days, and weeks. Most weather happens in the troposphere, the part of Earth's atmosphere that is closest to the ground.

Types of Weather

  • Tropical Cyclone
  • Tornado
  • Heat Wave
  • Rainy

What is a Climate?

Climate is the average weather in a given area over a longer period of time. A description of a climate includes information on, e.g., the average temperature in different seasons, rainfall, and sunshine. Also a description of the (chance of) extremes is often included. Climate change is any systematic change in the long-term statistics of climate variables such as temperature, precipitation, pressure, or wind, sustained over several decades or longer. Climate change can be due to natural external forcing (changes in solar emission or changes in the Earth's orbit) or to natural internal processes of the climate system, or it can be human-induced.

Types of Climate

  • Polar Climate
  • Continental Climate
  • Dessert
  • Tropical Rainforest Climate
