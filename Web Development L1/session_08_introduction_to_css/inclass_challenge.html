About the Author

Before talking about You Can Win Summary let's first talk the book's author Shiv Khera. He is an Indian author of self-help books and an activist. He launched a movement against caste-based reservation India. Founded an organization called country first foundation, and started Bhartiya Rashtravadi Samanta party. In 1998 Khera published his first book YOU CAN WIN (Jeet Aapki in Hindi). This book focuses on achieving success through personal growth and a positive attitude. Other books written by Khera is Living with Honor about living honorably and respectably in a fractured world.
